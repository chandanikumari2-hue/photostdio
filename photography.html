Niladri Das

For over 31 years, I’ve lived my life through a lens, capturing moments that are powerful, fleeting, and deeply human. My journey has taken me from the intensity of political rallies and army camps to the joy and intimacy of haldi, mehndi, and wedding celebrations each space shaping my vision with strength, patience, and emotion.

I’ve had the honor of working on the official photography team for the former President of India, Honorable Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, Honorable State Home Minister Nitya Nand Rai, Honorable Union Minister Of State Nitish Pramanik, and spent over two and a half years with the media team of Yogi Adityanath, where every frame demanded precision and responsibility. Through changing times and technology, one thing has never changed my passion for preserving real emotions, true stories, and timeless memories.

Every celebration is unique, and I believe your memories should reflect that. My team and I are passionate about preserving the essence of your special moments with warmth, authenticity, and cinematic beauty.

Travel Policy

• Local coverage within 25 km of our Office is included in all packages
• Outstation events: Travel and accommodation costs at actuals (economy flights, 3-star hotels)
• For destination weddiings, we provide a detailed travel estimate after location confirmation
• Team travels one day prior for multi-day events to ensure readiness

Payment Terms
